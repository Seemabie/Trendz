— what bespoke means here

Not a service. A collaboration.

Bespoke at Trendz is not a checkbox at the end of a sale. It is a six to twelve week conversation between you, our head designer, and a small workshop of karigars in Lahore who have been embroidering for thirty years.

You bring a color, an old family photograph, a song lyric, a feeling. We bring the silk, the thread, the silhouette, and the time. Together we make one piece that has never existed before and will never exist again.

No. 02 · The Process

Four steps from idea to wardrobe

The same path for a single piece or a whole trousseau. Honest at every stage, with no hidden costs and no surprise timelines.

01

The Consultation

Ninety minutes with our head designer. We talk about the event, your taste, your family, the photos saved on your phone. We pour chai.

02

The Sketch

Within ten days you receive two to three illustrated proposals with silk swatches and embroidery samples. You pick, refine, approve.

03

The Craft

Four to ten weeks in Lahore. We send you weekly photos so you watch your piece take shape. Every motif set by hand, no machines.

04

The Fitting

Two to three fittings in our Queens atelier. Every dart adjusted to the way you actually breathe and dance. Then you take it home.

No. 04 · The Process
— how a bridal piece is made

From the first stitch to the last fitting.

Week one is for talking and dreaming. We sit with you, your mother, your maid of honor. We sketch. We choose silk and zari. Week two through eight is for the karigars in Lahore, who set every motif by hand.

The final two weeks belong to you and our seamstresses in Queens. Three fittings, sometimes four. Every dart, every fall, every inch of dupatta tailored to the way you actually move.

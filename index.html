A House of Heirloom

Where the ancient art of South Asian couture meets the woman of today.

For decades, the bridal grounds of Lahore and the silk markets of Banaras have whispered legends of craftsmanship. Trendz Closet was born from a single conviction: that every woman deserves a piece of that legend in her own closet. We translate centuries of needlework, zardozi, and gota into garments tailored for the contemporary American wardrobe.

The Atelier

Imagine it. We will stitch it.

The dress in your mind. The fabric you adored at a wedding. The neckline from a film you watched on a slow Sunday. Bring us a reference, a fabric swatch, or simply a feeling, and we will commission it through our network of master ateliers in Lahore, Karachi, and Delhi.

Every commission begins with a private consultation, either at our New York atelier or over a video call. Sketches are shared. Samples are sent. Adjustments are unhurried. The garment arrives at your door.

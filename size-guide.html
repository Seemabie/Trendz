Size guide

Three measurements, a soft tape, and ten minutes. Read this before your fitting and you will be ahead of most brides.

No. 01 · How to Measure

Three measurements that matter most

Wear a thin, fitted layer. Stand naturally with your weight on both feet. Keep the tape snug but not tight. If you can, ask someone to help with the back measurements.

Bust

Around the fullest part of your chest, with the tape parallel to the floor. Breathe normally. Do not pull the tape tight.

Waist

The narrowest part of your torso, usually two inches above the belly button. Stand naturally, do not suck in.

Hip

Around the fullest part of your hips, about eight inches below the waist. Feet together, tape parallel to the floor.

A short note If you are between two sizes, size up. South Asian formalwear is built to be tailored down, not stretched up. We always include extra fabric in the seams so we can take a piece in by an inch or two without compromising the cut.
